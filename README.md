# 📊 Business KPI Decomposition & Growth Strategy Analysis

### SQL & Power BI project analyzing revenue drivers, customer retention, conversion behavior, and ecommerce growth opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project analyzes ecommerce business performance using SQL and Power BI to identify the key drivers impacting revenue growth and customer behavior.

The analysis focuses on decomposing business KPIs into actionable components such as:

- Traffic
- Conversion Rate
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Purchase Frequency
- Customer Retention
- Funnel Leakage
- Product Conversion Efficiency

The objective was to identify operational inefficiencies, customer drop-off patterns, and strategic growth opportunities using data-driven analysis.

---

## 📌 Business Objectives

This project was designed to answer key business questions:

### Revenue & KPI Decomposition
- Which business KPIs are driving revenue performance?
- Is revenue slowdown caused by traffic decline or conversion inefficiency?
- How does purchase frequency impact overall business growth?

### Funnel & Conversion Analysis
- Where do customers drop off in the ecommerce funnel?
- What is the overall conversion rate from view → purchase?
- How severe is cart abandonment behavior?

### Product & Category Performance
- Which products attract high traffic but fail to convert?
- Which categories demonstrate strong conversion efficiency?
- Which products contribute to revenue leakage?

### Customer Behavior & Retention
- Are customers demonstrating repeat purchase behavior?
- Which customer segments contribute most to business value?
- How effectively are users progressing through sessions?
